example. The coupon files were v3 then; regenerating today gives v4, which
already includes the LED correction below.

## 2026-10-25 — fit coupons v3, originally printed in ASA (exports/coupons-v3/)

The author: "Everything looks good in the test prints. Magnets fit well and so
does the housing and the buttons are fine. Only small issue is the indicator
light hole on the face plate needs to move left 1mm and up 0.5mm"

- Shell coupon: 0.8 mm skin, Ø11.6 button holes and the 0.15 mm/side keypad
  fit reported good.
- Post coupon: magnet pockets reported good. Which ridge step (0.15 / 0.25 /
  0.35 mm, 1/2/3 dots) felt best was not stated.
- LED hole at keypad-frame (19, 4) was 1 mm right and 0.5 mm low → corrected
  to (18, 3.5), MEASUREMENTS B11 (left/up read as seen from the front).
- Ridge step 2 (0.25 mm interference) felt best. The author: "Tightness 2 was best".
